Eve Irem is a creative technologist and technical artist working across immersive media, spatial computing and research-driven design.

Trained in architecture and engineering, they build experiential systems where real-time production meets conceptual inquiry, from LED-volume music videos to installations and research with physicists.

Their work has been shown at SIGGRAPH, NAB Show, the Venice Architecture Biennale, the Istanbul Design Biennial and CERN’s Collide programme, among others.
